# Haiku's Home 

An iOS app that aims to match stray animals with loving homes. 

Inspired by my work as a volunteer at a local cat rescue group where we organised TNR drives and adoptions for some of our community cats. 
Our rescue group used to have to use the community facebook group to spread the word about strays up for adoption, (which inevitably ended with our posts being buried under people selling furniture) so I built a dedicated platform for our animals. 

Built with SwiftUI + Firebase. Complete with user auth and an animal database with images.
The name came from our family dog, Haiku, who sadly passed away during its development.

> Note: Firebase recently changed from a free to a paid image storage plan, so images may not load correctly on the App Store Version! Working to resolve access now :)
